FAQ

Questions, answered

What exactly is in the pack vs the free export?

The free tier exports the standalone deal page as one self-contained HTML file you can host anywhere. The PRO campaign pack is a single ZIP download: index.html (the page), og.png (the matching share banner), launch-copy.txt (posts written from your offer, linking to your public page URL) and publish-checklist.txt (the pre-flight review as a checklist you can tick off).

Where do I host the page? Does it cost anything?

Anywhere that serves static files — and the best option is free: drag the downloaded file onto netlify.com/drop. One important detail: an unclaimed Drop site is temporary and password-protected — sign up for a free Netlify account right after dropping to claim your URL. GitHub Pages, Vercel, Cloudflare Pages, your own server — all fine too.

Which payment providers work as my checkout link?

Anything with a URL: Lemon Squeezy, Stripe payment links, Gumroad, Polar, Paddle, Whop… the buy button simply opens your link. You keep 100% of the payment relationship.

Does the tool change my checkout or disable my payment link?

No. BF Rocket never touches your payment provider — your checkout link is used as-is on the page's buy button. The pre-flight review asks you to open your checkout and confirm it charges the price you entered. And hiding the buy button on the page does not disable your payment link: only your provider controls what customers are actually charged.

What if my page URL isn't decided yet?

The launch posts are generated as drafts. Until you've published the page, each post carries a DRAFT label and a placeholder where the URL goes — publish the page, replace the placeholder with the real link, then post.

What happens when the countdown ends?

The countdown and the discounted price disappear, and the page shows a clean "the deal has ended — the price is back to normal" message. You can leave it running after the sale without embarrassment.

Do you store my product data or my logo?

No. Everything runs in your browser and saves to your own device's local storage. Your logo is embedded into your HTML file. There's no server on our side holding your data.

Can I use it again next year?

Yes — it's a one-time purchase, unlimited pages, forever. Update the prices next November and ship the same day.

Why a tool and not a template?

Because you'll fiddle with a template for three hours and still miss the countdown logic, the OG tags and the mobile layout. BF Rocket is opinionated on purpose: good defaults, zero decisions, live in 5 minutes.

Why not Carrd, Framer or Canva?

You'd still be making every design decision and hand-wiring countdown logic into a generic template. Canva gives you images, not a page. Countdown widgets need a site you already have. BF Rocket is the bundle: the page as one HTML file, the campaign as one download — decided for you.

Can I put the page on my own domain?

Yes — and you should for a big sale. Deploy with Netlify Drop, then in the Netlify dashboard open Domain settings → Add a domain and point the DNS records it shows you. Five minutes, free. Vercel and Cloudflare Pages work the same way.

What if something breaks on Friday night?

Your exported page is a plain HTML file — open it, click through it, host it on a free preview URL and test the whole flow before the sale. And if the tool itself fails you, the 30-day refund is unconditional (see the refund page).

Can I add product screenshots?

Yes — upload one product image and it's placed front and center on the page; your logo and accent color carry the brand everywhere else. Want more image slots? Tell us — it decides what gets built next.

What happens after I pay — and what if I lose my work?

Checkout isn't wired into this build yet — the launch price is the hypothesis we're testing, so nothing charges today. Your work autosaves in your browser, and “Save project” exports it as a file you can reload anywhere.
