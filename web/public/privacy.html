Privacy

Effective November 19, 2026

The short version

Nothing about what you build ever leaves your browser. The builder runs entirely in your browser; your product info, prices, logo and text live in your own device's local storage and in the files you export. There is no account and no database of your work.

What this site does collect

  • Basic, aggregated visit analytics (page views, referrers) to understand how the site is found. No ad networks, no cross-site tracking, no profiles.
  • Our hosting provider may set a short-lived technical session cookie for load balancing — we don't use it for tracking and can't switch it off.
  • The contest-mandated traffic tracker, once installed, records visit-level analytics required by the hackathon. It never sees what you build inside the tool.
  • The builder stores a small local event log (button clicks like "download", "open review") in your own browser's localStorage to help us improve the flow. It never leaves your device unless you choose to share it with us.
  • If you email us (for example via the "notify me when checkout is live" link), we receive your email address and use it only to reply. We don't add you to a marketing list without asking.

Payments

When checkout goes live, purchases will be processed by Lemon Squeezy as our merchant of record. They receive your payment details under their own privacy policy; we only receive your email address for license delivery and support. Until checkout is wired, nothing can be purchased and no payment data is collected.

License keys

When you activate PRO, the builder sends your license key plus a generated instance name to our license-validation endpoint (or Lemon Squeezy's license API) to check it. That is the only server interaction the product has. Your license key is also stored in your own browser's local storage after activation. Alternatively, the ⬇ Offline builder runs with no activation call at all.

Controller

The operator of this site is a solo founder operating under the name "BF Rocket". Full legal identity and contact details are published on this page before the first sale is processed. Until then, reach us at support@bfrocket.com (mailbox goes live with the domain) or through the operator's public launch channels.

Your rights

You can request access to or deletion of any personal data we hold (currently: only emails you have sent us) at the address above. Since the builder stores your work on your own device, deleting your browser data removes it completely — no copies exist on our side. Statutory consumer rights are unaffected.

License keys

Your license key is emailed to you by Lemon Squeezy and validated against their license API when you activate PRO. That's the only server interaction the product has.
